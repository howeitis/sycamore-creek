The mark grows in on first visit

Suggestion 10

Placements print in as you scroll

Each card's text first appears as thin brass bars of uneven height, like the butterfly built from bars in your motion channel. A print head then sweeps across and resolves them into letters. The cards go one after another as they scroll into view.
